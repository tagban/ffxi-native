Where is your game?

Choose the FINAL FANTASY XI folder from a Windows install (the one with FFXiMain.dll). The folder above it, PlayOnline or SquareEnix, works too. Nothing in it is changed.

Make the game for this computer

The launcher translates your copy of the game into a program for this computer. That happens here, once for each version of the game, and takes a minute or two. The translation never leaves this computer.

      While playing
      
        
      These change the game as it runs, within a second.
      
        
        
      Pressed alone in the game, it brings up this page over it (so does ⌘ ,; on a Mac keyboard, hold fn for F-keys). Pick one your key bindings don't use: the game never sees it.
      
        
      
          
          
        
      
        
        
      On a wider window (21:9), keeps the menus and text at this shape in the middle while the world fills the screen. Use a menu resolution of the same shape (960 × 540 for 16:9).
      
        
      
          
          
        
      
        
        
      In the game, Alt+Enter or ⌃⌘F switches between a window and full screen. The game keeps its resolution and is scaled to fit.
      
        
        
        
        
      The title and login screens play before the game's own volume setting applies: this
          keeps them quieter. In the world, the game's settings and the volume above apply.
      

        
      
          
      Scene effects
      
          
      Lighting and image effects drawn on top of the game's own. Off is the game as it shipped.
